# Subadmin Privacy Policy

Status: Draft for review  
Applies to: Subadmin portal, school management, student records, alumni approvals, donations/projects, monitoring, events, updates, and school page management.

This privacy policy explains how Subadmin-related personal data and school operational data are collected, used, stored, shared, and protected. This document should be reviewed by a legal professional before publishing.

## 1. Data We Collect

For Subadmin accounts, the platform may collect:

- Name, email, phone number, assigned school, role, and login credentials.
- OTP verification records and login/session data.
- Activity logs such as create, update, delete, approve, reject, reset access, and email trigger actions.
- Email communication logs.

Through Subadmin usage, the platform may process:

- Student data.
- School profile data.
- Alumni approval and verification data.
- Donation/project records.
- Event/gallery media.
- Updates and announcements.
- CSR and donation inquiry follow-up data.

## 2. Why We Collect Subadmin Data

Subadmin data is used to:

- Authenticate school administrators.
- Restrict access to assigned school data.
- Manage school operations.
- Track who performed important actions.
- Send OTPs and administrative emails.
- Support audit, monitoring, and accountability.
- Protect the platform from unauthorized access or misuse.

## 3. School Data Responsibility

Subadmins handle sensitive school-related data, especially student and alumni data. Subadmins must use the data only for authorized school/platform purposes.

Subadmins should not export, share, copy, misuse, or disclose school data outside approved school or Madni Education workflows.

## 4. Student and Alumni Data Handling

Subadmins may access student and alumni data for:

- Student management.
- Academic setup.
- Promotion.
- Needy/RTE/donation support.
- Alumni verification.
- Alumni access reset.
- Monitoring and communication.

This access must be used carefully because it can include personal, educational, financial-support, and contact information.

## 5. Sharing of Data

Subadmin data and school data may be visible to:

- Superadmin for global oversight.
- The assigned Subadmin and authorized school team.
- Alumni only where alumni-facing information is approved.
- Public website visitors only where content is approved for public display.
- Service providers needed for hosting, email, media, payment, security, and backups.

## 6. Security Controls

The platform should protect Subadmin data with:

- Password hashing.
- OTP login.
- Role-based access.
- School-based data filtering.
- HTTPS.
- Secure cookies/sessions.
- Redis-based OTP/rate limiting.
- Monitoring logs.
- Email logs.
- Database and server hardening.

## 7. Monitoring and Audit

Subadmin activity may be logged for accountability. Logs can include action type, actor, school, entity, status, timestamp, and related email logs.

Monitoring helps detect mistakes, misuse, failed emails, suspicious activity, and workflow history.

## 8. Retention

Subadmin account and activity records may be retained while the account exists and afterward where needed for school audit, legal compliance, dispute resolution, security, or operational history.

## 9. Contact

Subadmins should contact the Superadmin or official Madni Education platform owner for data correction, access issues, or privacy questions.

## 10. Reference Notes

This draft is written with awareness of Indian data-protection and website-policy expectations, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, Information Technology Act, 2000, and SPDI Rules, 2011. Final wording should be reviewed legally before launch.
