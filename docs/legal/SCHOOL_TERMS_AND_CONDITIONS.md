# School and Public Website Terms and Conditions

Status: Draft for review  
Applies to: Public website, school pages, donation inquiries, CSR inquiries, public alumni registration, school content, events, gallery, and public-facing information.

These terms explain the rules for using the public website and school-related public features. This document should be reviewed by a legal professional before publishing.

The website is provided to share information about Madni Education, connected schools, school activities, donation needs, alumni impact, CSR opportunities, and public registration/inquiry flows.

The website is not intended for misuse, false submissions, unauthorized access, or harmful communication.

## 2. Public Information

The platform tries to keep school, donation, event, and update information accurate. However, information may change over time and should be verified with the official school/admin contact where required.

## 3. Donation and Payment Terms

Visitors may submit donation inquiries or make payments where payment features are enabled.

Users are responsible for checking the selected school, project, amount, category, and payment details before submitting.

Payment success, failure, refund, receipt generation, and verification may depend on the payment provider and platform records.

## 4. CSR Inquiry Terms

CSR inquiry forms are used to express interest in supporting schools or projects. Submitting an inquiry does not create a binding agreement unless a separate written agreement is made with the trust/school.

CSR contacts should provide accurate company and contact details.

## 5. Public Alumni Registration

Old students may submit alumni registration from the public flow. Submission does not guarantee alumni portal access.

The relevant school Subadmin must verify and approve the request before alumni credentials are issued.

## 6. Prohibited Use

Users must not:

- Submit fake inquiries.
- Impersonate another person or organization.
- Attempt unauthorized login or access.
- Upload or submit abusive, false, illegal, obscene, hateful, or harmful content.
- Interfere with website security or performance.
- Misuse donation, CSR, alumni, or school forms.
- Scrape, copy, or misuse data without permission.

Those services may have their own terms and privacy policies. Users should review them where relevant.

The platform is provided to support school, alumni, donation, and public information workflows. Madni Education should not be responsible for losses caused by misuse, incorrect user-submitted information, third-party service failure, or unauthorized activity beyond reasonable platform control.

These terms may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the website after updates means acceptance of the updated terms.

## 11. Contact

For questions about school content, donation inquiries, CSR inquiries, alumni registration, or these terms, users should contact the official Madni Education or school contact published on the website.
