# Alumni Terms and Conditions

Status: Draft for review  
Applies to: Alumni portal, alumni registration, alumni profile, community feed, posts, donations, CSR referrals, mentorship, career opportunities, and alumni communications.

These terms explain the rules for using the Alumni portal. This document should be reviewed by a legal professional before publishing.

## 1. Eligibility

The Alumni portal is only for verified alumni or approved old students of schools connected with Madni Education.

Creating an alumni registration request does not guarantee access. The relevant school Subadmin must approve the request before credentials are issued.

## 2. Account Responsibility

Alumni are responsible for:

- Providing accurate registration and profile information.
- Keeping login credentials private.
- Not sharing OTPs or passwords with others.
- Updating profile details when necessary.
- Immediately reporting suspicious account activity.

## 3. Acceptable Use

Alumni must use the portal for genuine alumni, school, community, donation, mentorship, career, and CSR-related purposes.

Alumni must not:

- Create fake accounts.
- Impersonate another person.
- Upload false, abusive, harmful, obscene, illegal, discriminatory, or misleading content.
- Share another person's private information without permission.
- Spam users or misuse communication features.
- Attempt to bypass security, access other accounts, or interfere with the platform.
- Use the platform for fraud, unlawful fundraising, or unauthorized commercial activity.

## 4. Content Submission and Moderation

Alumni may submit stories, achievements, jobs, internships, mentorship offers, blogs, CSR referrals, and other content.

Madni Education or the relevant school admin may approve, reject, edit visibility, hide, or remove content if it is inaccurate, inappropriate, irrelevant, harmful, or against platform rules.

## 5. Public Content

Approved alumni content may be shown inside the Alumni portal and, where selected, on the public website.

By submitting content, alumni confirm they have the right to share it and allow Madni Education to display it for school, community, alumni, and impact-related purposes.

## 6. Donations and Payments

Donation and contribution features are provided to support schools, students, projects, and approved causes.

Alumni must ensure payment details are correct before submitting. Payment success, failure, refunds, and receipts may depend on the payment provider and platform verification.

## 7. Mentorship and Career Posts

Mentorship offers, jobs, and internships should be genuine and accurate. Alumni posting opportunities are responsible for the truthfulness of the information they provide.

Madni Education may remove opportunities that are misleading, expired, unsafe, or not suitable for the community.

## 8. Account Suspension or Removal

Madni Education or school admins may suspend, restrict, or remove alumni access if:

- The account is fake or unverifiable.
- The account violates these terms.
- The user misuses the platform.
- There is a security or legal concern.
- The school determines the user is not an approved alumni.

## 9. No Guarantee

The platform is provided to support alumni engagement and school development. Madni Education does not guarantee job placement, mentorship outcomes, donation outcomes, uninterrupted access, or acceptance of every submitted post.

## 10. Changes to Terms

These terms may be updated as the platform grows. Continued use of the Alumni portal after updates means the alumni accepts the updated terms.

## 11. Contact

For account, content, or terms-related questions, alumni should contact the school Subadmin or official platform admin contact.
