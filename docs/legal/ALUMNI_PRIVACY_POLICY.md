# Alumni Privacy Policy

Status: Draft for review  
Applies to: Alumni portal, alumni login, alumni registration, community posts, donations, CSR referrals, mentorship, career posts, and alumni profile features.

This privacy policy explains how Madni Education collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects alumni data. This document should be reviewed by a legal professional before publishing.

## 1. Data We Collect

The Alumni portal may collect:

- Name, email address, phone number, and login details.
- School name, batch year, student history, and alumni verification details.
- Profile details such as current title, bio, LinkedIn/profile links, photos, and achievements.
- Community content such as stories, jobs, internships, mentorship posts, blogs, comments, likes, and activity.
- Donation and contribution details, including selected project/category, amount, transaction status, receipts, and payment reference IDs.
- CSR referral details such as company name, contact person, email, phone, and message.
- Security and system logs such as login attempts, OTP verification, IP-related request metadata, device/browser metadata where available, and activity logs.

## 2. Why We Collect Alumni Data

We use alumni data to:

- Verify alumni identity and authorize access.
- Allow alumni to log in securely using password and OTP.
- Maintain alumni profiles and school connection.
- Show approved alumni content in the alumni portal or public website.
- Support alumni community, mentorship, career opportunities, and networking.
- Process donations, donation history, receipts, and contribution tracking.
- Process CSR referrals and follow-up communication.
- Send important emails such as OTPs, credential emails, password reset emails, invitations, and meeting links.
- Monitor misuse, fraud, unauthorized access, spam, or harmful content.
- Improve platform reliability, reporting, and user experience.

## 3. Legal and Consent Basis

Alumni data is processed for lawful platform purposes such as account access, school verification, alumni services, donation processing, communication, and security.

Where consent is required, the platform should request clear consent before collecting or using personal data. Alumni can choose not to provide optional profile or public content, but some required data is necessary for login, verification, and account use.

## 4. Alumni Approval and Authorization

Old-student registration does not automatically give alumni access. The relevant school Subadmin verifies and approves the request. Only after approval does the system generate alumni access and email credentials.

This protects the alumni community from fake or unauthorized accounts.

## 5. Public Visibility of Alumni Content

Some alumni content may be visible to other alumni or public website visitors after approval, including:

- Name and batch year.
- Profile summary.
- Achievements and stories.
- Mentorship offers.
- Career posts.
- Alumni spotlight details.

Sensitive personal data, login credentials, OTPs, private donation metadata, and internal verification data should not be publicly displayed.

## 6. Sharing of Data

Alumni data may be shared internally with:

- Superadmin for global monitoring and management.
- The relevant school Subadmin for approval, moderation, support, and monitoring.
- Payment provider for payment processing where donations are made.
- Email provider for sending OTPs, credentials, password resets, invitations, and communication.
- Media provider such as ImageKit for uploaded images/media.
- Hosting/database providers required to operate the platform.

Data should not be sold to third parties.

## 7. Security

The platform should protect alumni data using:

- Password hashing.
- OTP verification.
- Redis-based OTP expiry and rate limiting.
- HTTPS.
- Secure cookies/session handling.
- Role-based access control.
- Database access restrictions.
- Monitoring and email logs.
- Server firewall and backup controls.

## 8. Data Retention

Alumni records may be retained while the alumni account is active or while required for school history, donation records, legal compliance, audit, or legitimate trust/school records.

Alumni may request correction or account/data review through the school or platform admin contact.

## 9. Alumni Rights and Requests

Alumni may request:

- Correction of inaccurate profile data.
- Review of account data.
- Password reset or access support.
- Removal of optional public profile content where reasonable.
- Clarification on how their data is used.

Some records, such as donation receipts, audit logs, or legal records, may need to be retained.

## 10. Contact

For privacy requests, alumni should contact the school Subadmin or the official Madni Education admin contact published on the website.

## 11. Reference Notes

This draft is written with awareness of Indian data-protection and website-policy expectations, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, Information Technology Act, 2000, and SPDI Rules, 2011. Final wording should be reviewed legally before launch.
