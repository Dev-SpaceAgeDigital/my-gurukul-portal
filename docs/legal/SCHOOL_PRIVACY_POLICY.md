# School and Public Website Privacy Policy

Status: Draft for review  
Applies to: School pages, public website, student support/donation needs, public inquiries, CSR inquiries, donation forms, event/gallery content, and school-related public content.

This privacy policy explains how school-related and public website data is collected, used, stored, shared, and protected. This document should be reviewed by a legal professional before publishing.

## 1. Data We Collect

The public website and school modules may collect or display:

- School name, address, contact details, trust relation, facilities, programs, and public profile.
- School events, gallery images, updates, announcements, and public page content.
- Donation inquiry details such as donor name, email, phone, selected category/project, amount, and message.
- Payment-related data such as order ID, payment ID, transaction status, and receipt details.
- CSR inquiry details such as company name, contact person, email, phone, and support message.
- Public alumni registration details where old students register from the website.
- Technical data such as request metadata, security logs, and abuse-prevention records.

## 2. Student Data on Public Website

The platform should avoid showing private student personal data publicly unless there is lawful permission and a clear approved purpose.

Donation needs may show category, standard, project, or support need, but sensitive child/student information should be minimized and protected.

## 3. Why We Collect Public and School Data

Data is used to:

- Display school information publicly.
- Show real school updates, events, and impact.
- Process donation inquiries and payment flows.
- Generate receipts and donation records.
- Receive CSR interest and follow-up.
- Support public alumni registration.
- Improve transparency and trust.
- Protect the website from misuse, spam, fraud, and unauthorized access.

## 4. Public Visibility

The website may publicly show:

- School profile and trust information.
- Approved school events and gallery images.
- Approved updates and announcements.
- Public donation needs or projects.
- Public financial transparency content.
- Approved alumni stories, achievements, and spotlight content.

Private admin data, OTPs, passwords, internal logs, private student records, and unapproved alumni data should not be publicly displayed.

## 5. Sharing of Data

Public inquiry and school data may be shared with:

- Superadmin.
- Relevant school Subadmin.
- Payment provider for donation/payment processing.
- Email provider for confirmations and follow-ups.
- Media provider for uploaded images.
- Hosting/database/security providers needed to run the website.

Data should not be sold to third parties.

## 6. Cookies and Technical Data

The website may use technical cookies or session mechanisms for login, security, and platform operation. Public analytics or tracking tools should be disclosed if added later.

## 7. Security

The platform should use:

- HTTPS.
- Secure server configuration.
- Role-based admin access.
- Input validation.
- Rate limiting.
- SQL injection prevention through parameterized queries.
- XSS-safe rendering.
- Secure payment provider integration.
- Backups and monitoring.

## 8. Retention

School public content may remain visible while it is relevant. Donation, CSR, receipt, and inquiry records may be retained for audit, legal, operational, or accounting purposes.

## 9. Contact

Visitors, donors, CSR contacts, parents, alumni, and school stakeholders can contact the official school or Madni Education contact for privacy-related questions.

## 10. Reference Notes

This draft is written with awareness of Indian data-protection and website-policy expectations, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, Information Technology Act, 2000, SPDI Rules, 2011, and website guidance that public-facing websites should publish privacy policy and terms/user agreement. Final wording should be reviewed legally before launch.
