# Subadmin Terms and Conditions

Status: Draft for review  
Applies to: Subadmin portal and all school-level administrative activity.

These terms explain the rules for school Subadmins using the platform. This document should be reviewed by a legal professional before publishing.

## 1. Authorized Use

The Subadmin portal is only for authorized school administrators assigned by Superadmin.

Subadmin access is school-specific. A Subadmin must manage only the school assigned to them and must not attempt to access another school's data.

## 2. Account Responsibility

Subadmins are responsible for:

- Keeping login credentials confidential.
- Not sharing OTPs or passwords.
- Using only official/admin email accounts.
- Logging out from shared devices.
- Reporting suspicious activity immediately.

## 3. Data Responsibility

Subadmins may handle student, alumni, donation, school, CSR, and communication data. This data must be used only for legitimate school/platform work.

Subadmins must not:

- Misuse student or alumni data.
- Download/export data for unauthorized use.
- Share private records publicly.
- Approve fake alumni.
- Reset alumni access without valid reason.
- Delete or modify records dishonestly.
- Upload misleading public content.

## 4. Student Management Rules

Student records should be accurate and updated responsibly. Imports, edits, promotions, and bulk actions should be checked carefully before submission.

Incorrect student data can affect reports, donations, alumni conversion, and school decision-making.

## 5. Alumni Approval Rules

Subadmins must verify old-student requests before approval. Credentials should be sent only after reasonable school verification.

Approving unknown or fake users can create security and privacy risks for the alumni community.

## 6. Public Content Rules

Subadmins may manage updates, events, school page content, project media, and gallery images. Public content should be truthful, respectful, school-relevant, and approved for public display.

Subadmins should not upload content that violates privacy, copyright, child safety, dignity, or applicable law.

## 7. Donations and Project Records

Donation/project records should be entered accurately. Amounts, categories, standards, project status, media, and ledgers must not be manipulated.

Financial records should be handled with extra care because donors, schools, and Superadmin may rely on them.

## 8. Monitoring

Subadmin actions may be logged and reviewed by Superadmin. This includes data changes, approvals, access resets, email triggers, and content actions.

Monitoring exists for accountability, support, and security.

## 9. Suspension or Removal

Superadmin may suspend or remove Subadmin access if:

- The Subadmin leaves the school.
- Access is misused.
- Credentials are compromised.
- Unauthorized data sharing occurs.
- The account violates these terms.
- There is a security or legal risk.

## 10. Changes to Terms

These terms may be updated as platform operations grow. Continued use of the Subadmin portal after updates means acceptance of the updated terms.
